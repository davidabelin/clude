The **Classic board** is the board [[clude]] has been played on since 15 September 2026: a grid of 24 by 25 squares with nine rooms round a central cellar, seventeen doors, and two **secret passages** joining the rooms in opposite corners. A player rolls one die and must use the whole roll unless the move ends in a room.[^board]

Before that date the game was played on a simpler **ring board**, the nine rooms joined in a circle by short corridors. Games on the ring were shorter, and every measurement made on it was repeated on the Classic board; where an article quotes a ring-era figure it says so.[^history]
