The **Classic board** is the board used by [[clude]] since 15 September 2026. Its grid has 24 by 25 squares, nine rooms around a central cellar, seventeen doors and two **secret passages** connecting opposite corners. Movement uses one die. A player must use the full roll unless the move ends in a room.[^board]

The earlier **ring board** connected the nine rooms in a circle with short corridors. Its games were generally shorter. Major benchmarks and arena comparisons were repeated on the Classic board; articles identify historical ring-board results separately.[^history]
