**clude** is a web app for playing [[Clue]] at a table of people and characters. Each of the six suspects is a [[Category:Characters|character]] with a way of reasoning of its own, six real and different [[Category:Methods|methods]] from probability and machine learning, all standing on one shared [[deduction floor]]. A character can play in silence by its numbers, or with [[Claude]] in the seat to give it a voice, table talk and a memory. The name is a nod to Claude.[^readme]

It is a private project by David Abelin, made for family and friends, and this encyclopaedia is part of it.
