**clude** is a web app for playing [[Clue]] with people, numerical characters and chat agents. Each of the six suspects has a different [[Category:Methods|method]] for estimating hidden cards, constrained by the shared [[deduction floor]]. Characters can play silently as headless seats, or with [[Claude]] choosing among scored options and supplying a voice. Supported methods have numerical memory, and model-piloted seats can keep narrative logbooks when remembering is enabled. The name is a nod to Claude.[^readme]

It is a private project by David Abelin, made for family and friends, and this encyclopaedia is part of it.
