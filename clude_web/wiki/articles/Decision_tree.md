A **decision tree** is the method by which [[Colonel Mustard]] forms his [[belief]]. It is a [[w:Decision tree learning|tree of yes-or-no questions]] about a card (how many players could still hold it, how many times it has been named and by how many different players, how far the game has run) ending in a number: the share of cards in past games that gave the same answers and turned out to be in [[the envelope]]. The tree is grown, not written: it is fitted to the records of games already played, and with a [[logbook]] it is regrown from every game since.[^glossary]

It needs almost no time to consult, and it is only as good as its past is like the present.
