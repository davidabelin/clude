The **belief benchmark** measures how good each method's numbers are, apart from how the character then plays. {{fact:bench.grid.games}} games are played out between purely logical players; at four points in each, when a quarter, a half, three-quarters and all of the game's [[suggestion|suggestions]] have been made, every method is shown the same position from each seat and its [[belief]] is scored against the envelope's true contents by [[log-loss]]. That gives {{fact:bench.grid.snapshots}} positions in all.[^glossary]

The results on the [[Classic board]] are tabulated at [[Naive Bayes#Measured|Naive Bayes]] and discussed for one method at [[Professor Plum#Record|Professor Plum]].
