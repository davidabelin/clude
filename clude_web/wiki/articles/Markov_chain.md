A **Markov chain** is the method by which [[Mrs. White]] forms her [[belief]]. She reduces each opponent's history of [[suggestion|suggestions]] to a string of two symbols, *repeat* (this suggestion names a card that player has named before) and *new*, and fits to it a [[w:Markov chain|two-state Markov chain]]: a model in which what comes next depends only on what came last. A player in a run of repeats is read as still fishing for cards they have not been shown, and the cards they keep naming become more suspect.[^glossary]

It is the one method of the six that takes its evidence from how the other players behave, not from who showed what.
