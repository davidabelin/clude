**Dempster-Shafer theory** is the method by which [[Mrs. Peacock]] forms her [[belief]]. Where a probability gives each card one number, the theory gives it two: its *belief*, the weight of evidence that points to that card and no other, and its *plausibility*, the weight of evidence that does not rule it out. Evidence that says only "one of these three" can be held as exactly that, a weight on the set of three, without being shared out among them. Pieces of evidence are combined by [[w:Dempster–Shafer theory|Dempster's rule]].[^glossary]

In clude each open "this player holds at least one of these cards" fact from the [[deduction floor]] is one piece of evidence. Peacock reports a single probability for each card when one is needed, and keeps the two bounds; she accuses on the lower one.
