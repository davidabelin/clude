The **landing rule** is a change made on 18 September 2026 to how every character values a place it could move to. Until then, ending a move in any room scored full marks, since a room is where a [[suggestion]] can be made. That rewarded reaching a room whose card was already known to be in another player's hand, where any suggestion would simply be answered with the room. Characters with a [[Classic board|secret passage]] to hand rode it back and forth, repeating themselves.[^glossary]

Under the rule such a room counts only as a step on the way to a useful one. Games became shorter on every table measured, and exact repeats of a character's own suggestions fell by two-thirds or more. The episode is told at [[Professor Plum#The parking|Professor Plum]], who was the worst affected.
