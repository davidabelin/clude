The **landing rule** is a movement-score change introduced on 18 September 2026. The earlier score rewarded arriving in any room, even when its card was known to be in another player's hand and could readily disprove a [[suggestion]]. This encouraged some characters to shuttle through [[Classic board|secret passages]] and repeat answered questions.[^glossary]

The revised score treats such a room mainly as a step towards a useful destination. In the recorded comparisons, games became shorter and repeated suggestions fell substantially. The rule changes movement incentives; it does not prohibit entering a resolved room. [[Professor Plum#The parking|Professor Plum]]'s article describes the behaviour that prompted it.
