A **logbook** stores a character's narrative memory between games. With remembering enabled, a model-piloted seat receives the revealed deal after a game and writes an entry in its [[persona|voice]]: events, observations about opponents, lessons and standing instructions. Before a later game, the *memory* setting controls how much of that logbook is included in the model's context.[^logbooks]

[[Colonel Mustard]], [[Mrs. White]] and [[Mr. Green]] also have numerical method memory. It can be used by headless as well as model-piloted seats and is distinct from narrative entries. The documented extended logbook experiment is described at [[Professor Plum#Memory|Professor Plum]].
