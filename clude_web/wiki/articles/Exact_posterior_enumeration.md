**Exact posterior enumeration** is the method by which [[Professor Plum]] forms his [[belief]]. It lists every way the hidden cards could be dealt that agrees with everything seen so far, and takes a card's probability to be the share of those deals that put it in [[the envelope]]. When the list can be completed the result is exactly right; when it cannot, because early in a game there are billions of deals, the method falls back on a random sample of {{code:plum.sample_budget}} of them.[^glossary]

A worked example, the search and its budgets, and what the measurements found are for the present described under [[Professor Plum#How he thinks|Professor Plum]]. The same example is worked by a different method at [[Naive Bayes]].
