Privacy

clude is a game for family and friends. This is everything it keeps, and where.

Your account

Your name, a hash of your password (never the password itself), and the look you picked. Accounts are created manually; nobody signs up.

The games

Every game played here is kept, with no end date: who sat where, every move, suggestion, accusation and card shown, what was said at the table, and what the model seats cost. Anyone signed in can replay a finished game.

The characters remember. After a game a character may write notes about it, and you, to read back into memory at later tables.

Who else sees it

When a table has a character played by Claude, what that seat can see of the game, the table talk and its notes are sent to Anthropic's API to choose its moves and lines. A Claude in a chat window that plays a seat reads the table the same way a player does.

clude runs on Google Cloud, in the United States: the app on Cloud Run (us-central1), the games and accounts in Cloud Storage.

Cookies and your device

One cookie, to keep you signed in. No analytics, no advertising, nothing from a third party: even the fonts are served by clude. Your browser keeps a few settings of yours on your own device, such as whether sound is on.

Leaving

Request at Contact and your account is deleted. The games you played stay in the record, under your name.
