The deduction floor, and where every method sits

CLAUDE.md's first invariant, as a picture: the floor masks every belief before it is used. Agents differ in how they reason under uncertainty, never in what is logically certain. Six methods, one gate in front of them and one after. Every method article needs this one.

Inside the floor: seeding and the fixpoint

What propagate() does to one seat's observation. The four seeding steps run once; the three rules then run in a loop until a pass changes nothing. Rule labels are read from the methods' own docstrings, so they cannot drift. Recomputed from scratch every call -- no agent can act on a stale mask.

Scarlett: the multiplicative fold

Her whole method. Three kinds of suggestion, two soft updates, then the same mask and renormalise every method ends with. The two factors are read live from naive_bayes.py.

Scarlett: the independence assumption, and what it costs

The left panel is the net her arithmetic implies -- each suggestion an independent witness, no edges between them. The right is the same three suggestions when one player refuted all three: they are coupled, because a single card in that hand can answer all of them. Scarlett multiplies her decay three times as if she had learned three separate things. The missing edges are the character.
